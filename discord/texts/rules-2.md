When a rule is broken
We start with a reminder. After that we may remove a message, pause your ability to post for a while or remove you from the server. Serious harm skips the first steps.

### To report something

Press and hold a message or right-click it, choose Apps, then Report to moderators. You can also type {command:report}. Every {role:Moderator} can see a report and who sent it. To report a member of staff, write to {contact_email} instead. The Code of Conduct names that address and is a link that works: <https://github.com/phaemos/phaemos/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md>. We aim to look at every report within two days.

### To question a decision

Write to {contact_email}, which the Code of Conduct also names: <https://github.com/phaemos/phaemos/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md>. A different person looks at it where that is possible.
