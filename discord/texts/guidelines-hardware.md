Before you post
- Check the wiring tables and the parts list in the documentation. /hardware links to them.
- One build question in each post.

In your post
- The board and its revision, the sensors or parts and how they are powered.
- What you expected and what you measured. Numbers help.
- Photos are welcome. Describe each one in a sentence so that everyone can follow, including
  people who cannot see the picture. Add the description with the Alt button when you attach it.

Safety first: if mains voltage or a moving machine is involved, say so. We may ask you to stop
and check before going further.

When it is solved, add the Solved tag.
