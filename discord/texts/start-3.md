Good to know
- This server is quiet by default. You are only notified when someone mentions you. To hear about every release, open {channel:releases}, open its notification settings and choose all messages.
- You can change which channels you see at any time in {nav:customize}.
- Type / to see our commands. {command:help} lists them.
- To report a message to the moderators, press and hold it or right-click it, choose Apps, then Report to moderators.
- Alerts from your own machines belong in a channel of your own. This server does not receive them.
- What happens to personal data here: <https://phaemos.com/privacy>
